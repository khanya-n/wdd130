Trips

Morning Madness

An early morning trip for energetic folks who like to start their day with an exciting trip down the Vaal River. This trip is typically fast-paced and fewer breaks are taken, so be prepared to go all-out!

All-Day Experience

A more relaxing trip for folks who prefer to take things slow and appreciate the natural beauty of the area. Bring plenty of food and drinks along, as you will be out for many hours and will need to stay energized enough to complete the journey!

Sunset Sensation

An evening trip where you can experience sunset in a new way, as you embark on this fairly short journey. It is meant for folks who want a more casual trip and prefer to go sightseeing as the sun goes down to signal the end of another day.

Available Trips Details
Morning Madness Time: 08:00 to 11:00 Price: R400 per adult, R180 per child (under 13 years old)
All-Day Experience Time: 09:00 to 17:00 Price: R1000 per adult, R450 per child (under 13 years old)
Sunset Sensation Time: 16:00 to 19:00 Price: R500 per adult, R225 per child (under 13 years old)
